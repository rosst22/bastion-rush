Bastion Rush Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Bastion Rush works without an account. It does not request your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier. It has no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Game data

Coins, upgrades, settings, and scores are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app may delete this progress.

Purchases

If you buy the Commander Pack, Apple processes payment and RevenueCat validates the transaction and restores access. Purchase history is used for app functionality and aggregate purchase analytics. Bastion Rush uses an anonymous app identifier and does not receive your complete payment card number.

Children

Bastion Rush is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Retention and choices

Local data remains until you reset progress in the app or delete the app. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase records under their respective policies. Because there is no Bastion Rush account, we generally cannot identify or delete device-local data remotely.
