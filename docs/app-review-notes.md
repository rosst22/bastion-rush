# App Review Notes

Bastion Rush is a single-player, portrait squad tactics game. No account or login is required. All gameplay works offline; internet access is used only to load and validate the optional in-app purchase.

To reach the in-app purchase:

1. Complete or skip through the three onboarding pages.
2. On the home screen, tap the “Commander Pack” banner near the bottom.
3. The paywall clearly identifies a one-time purchase and includes Restore Purchases, Privacy, and Terms.

The Commander Pack is a non-consumable purchase that unlocks the Ember and Ivory cosmetic squad colors plus a supporter badge. It provides no gameplay advantage. To view squad colors, open Armory from the home screen and scroll to Squad Colors.

Product ID: `com.rosstoma.bastionrush.commanderpack`
RevenueCat entitlement: `commander_pack`

The app contains no advertisements, external purchase links, Stripe checkout, account creation, analytics SDK, tracking, user-generated content, chat, or loot boxes. Stylized combat uses toy-like shapes and contains no blood or gore.

No review account is required.
